CONNECT LANDING PAGE LEADS TO THE MIGRIZO CRM
=============================================

1. UPLOAD TO GITHUB (landing page repository), replacing the existing files:
     app/api/gtv-lead/route.ts
     README.md
     .env.example
   (If you have not uploaded app/layout.tsx from "migrizo-upload-fix" yet, upload that too.)

2. COPY THE CRM'S SECRET
   Netlify > open the CRM site (crm.migrizo.com) > Site configuration >
   Environment variables > INGEST_SECRET > copy its value.
   (Do not paste it anywhere else, and do not change it: Make.com uses it too.)

3. ADD IT TO THE LANDING PAGE SITE
   Netlify > open the landing page site (gtv.migrizo.com) > Site configuration >
   Environment variables > Add a variable:
     Key:   CRM_INGEST_SECRET
     Value: (the value you copied)
   Then Deploys > Trigger deploy > Deploy site.

4. TEST
   Open https://gtv.migrizo.com/ , complete the quiz with your own details.
   Within seconds the lead appears in the CRM Leads list with:
     Visa: GTV   Source: GTV Landing Page (or Google Ads when it came from an ad)
     Tags: gtv-landing (+ gtv-qualified if the score is 60 or more)
     Intake answers: score, band, experience, role, evidence, timeline, LinkedIn, gclid, UTMs
   Delete the test lead afterwards (CRM and the gtv_leads table in Supabase).

Note: new leads with an email get the CRM's automatic "How it works" email,
same as Meta and website leads.

Do not upload this READ-ME-FIRST.txt file.
